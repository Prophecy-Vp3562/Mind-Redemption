# Mind Redemption

> *A private sanctuary for honest thought, deep clarity, and quiet reflection.*

---

In a digital world filled with notifications, cloud syncs, and prying algorithms, **Mind Redemption** was built to be different: a personal, quiet room on your own computer. 

Here, your thoughts belong entirely to you. There are no accounts to create, no servers watching your keystrokes, and no subscriptions. Just you, your thoughts, and a warm, tactile space to think without judgment or distraction.

---

## 🛡️ Your Privacy is Absolute

We believe that true introspection requires complete safety.

* **100% Offline & Local**: Everything you write stays strictly inside your computer's Documents folder (`~/Documents/Mind Redemption Data`).
* **Zero Cloud & Zero Tracking**: No analytics, no telemetry, no cookies, and no background pings to the internet.
* **Invisible Vault**: Keep your inner thoughts truly private. What appears on your screen can remain benign, while your deeper reflections stay protected under your own key.
* **Chameleon Masking**: Writing in a public cafe or library? Tap `Ctrl + ` ` to instantly cloak your screen with classical prose as you type. Your genuine words are saved safely in the background, invisible to anyone looking over your shoulder.

---

## 🌿 A Space Designed for Mental Peace

Mind Redemption adapts to how your mind naturally organizes ideas:

* **Mind Flow (The Thinking Canvas)**: Anchor a central insight and examine it honestly from all angles—assumptions, counterarguments, emotional context, and consequences.
* **Daily Reflections (Diary)**: Check in with yourself each evening. Capture how the day felt and what demanded your attention.
* **All-Time Library (Notes)**: A timeless wall of your personal insights, cards, and ideas that you can search and revisit whenever you need perspective.
* **Gentle Editorial Themes**:
  * **Nordic Sage (Light)**: Warm stone mist, soft lichen tones, and peat graphite ink that feels like writing on archival paper in morning light.
  * **Smoked Obsidian (Dark)**: Deep charcoal slate and soft amber accents that let you write late into the night without eye strain.

## 🚀 Getting Started in 60 Seconds

You do not need to be a developer to make this space your own.

### The Easy Way (Windows)

1. **Download & Unzip** this project to your favorite folder.
2. Ensure you have **[Python](https://www.python.org/downloads/)** installed (make sure to check the box that says *"Add Python to PATH"* during setup).
3. **Double-click `run.bat`**.
   * On your first run, it quietly prepares everything automatically.
   * Your browser will open to your brand new private desk: `http://localhost:3000`.
4. When you are done writing, double-click **`stop.bat`** to cleanly close the session.
